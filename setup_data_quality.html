Data Quality · Account preparation

Create your Data Quality training account

Prepare for the Data Quality exercises by registering for an Informatica Cloud Data Quality trial and checking that you can access the service.

Informatica currently advertises a free 30-day trial. Check the trial terms when you register and plan your exercises within the available trial period.

Open the official trial page ↗

Before you start

  • Have access to your work email inbox.
  • Prepare your own name, organization, phone number, and location details.
  • Choose a data center location appropriate for your location and any organization data residency requirements.

Values in braces, such as {your work email}, are placeholders. Replace them with your own details on the Informatica registration form.

1. Open the trial page

1
Open Cloud Data Quality registration.

Follow the official Cloud Data Quality trial link. Confirm that the page is for Cloud Data Quality and locate the registration form.

Use this field guide while completing the form on Informatica’s website. Fields and available options may vary.

2
Enter your own details in all required fields.

Check the spelling of your email address and confirm your username choice before continuing.

3. Choose a data center location

3
Select an appropriate data center location.

Use the Data Center Location dropdown. This determines where your service instance runs and where metadata, and data for some services, is stored.

For example, choose Asia Pacific if it is available and appropriate for your location. Follow your organization’s location requirements.

4. Review and submit

4
Review your details and consent choices.

Read the subscription agreement and accept it if you agree. Make your own choice about the separate marketing communications checkbox.

5
Select Start your FREE trial.

Complete any verification prompts and follow the confirmation instructions shown by Informatica. If a field is highlighted, correct it before submitting again.

5. Activate your account and sign in

6
Follow the instructions from Informatica.

Check the confirmation page and your email inbox for the next steps. If an activation email is sent, follow its link to open the account creation page.

Keep your password private and save your sign-in details in your password manager.

7
Set your new Informatica account password.

When the activation link opens Create Your Account, complete the following fields:

  1. Check that Your User Name shows the username you registered.
  2. Enter your own new password in Your New Password. Follow the password requirements displayed on the page.
  3. Enter the same password in Confirm New Password.
  4. Select Your Security Question and enter your own answer in Your Security Question’s Answer.
  5. Choose whether to receive marketing communications, then click Submit.

Wait for confirmation that account creation is complete, then sign in with your username and new password. Keep your password and security answer private.

8
Sign in to your trial environment.

Use the sign-in link provided during registration or in your welcome email. Bookmark the environment URL so you can return for the exercises.

6. Check you are ready for the exercises

9
Confirm access to Cloud Data Quality.

After signing in, look for the Data Quality service in your available services and open it. If access is still being provisioned, follow any status message before trying again.

Account setup complete — use this environment for your self-paced Data Quality exercises.

Troubleshooting

I have not received an email

Check spam and junk folders and verify the email address you submitted. Follow any resend or support option provided on the confirmation page.

The username or email is already registered

Follow the form’s message. If the account belongs to you, use the sign-in or account recovery option. Check whether your existing account has Data Quality access. If trial eligibility or access is unclear, use the support options on the trial page.

I cannot see the Data Quality service

Confirm you registered through the Cloud Data Quality trial page and signed in to the environment from your welcome instructions. Follow any provisioning message, then contact Informatica support if access remains unavailable.

My form looks different

Use the labels on the live registration form. Complete required fields with your own details. Use the field help text to choose the appropriate region and organization options, or use the page’s support options if needed.
